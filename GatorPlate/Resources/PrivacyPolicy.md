# GatorPlate Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-10-draft-1
**Last updated:** October 16, 2026
**Status:** Draft placeholder. The final policy will replace this text before release.

---

## Key points
- You can browse GatorPlate and get alerts **without an account**. Guests get an anonymous ID, with no name or email.
- An account (email, name, account type) is needed **only to post** food.
- Your **location stays on your phone**. It is never stored or shared.
- Alert choices such as diet or allergens are **visible only to you** and are never used to target anyone.
- We **never sell** your data and never track what food you pick up.

---

## 1. What we collect
- **Guests:** an anonymous ID created by Firebase, the Terms version you accepted, and your alert settings if you turn alerts on.
- **Accounts:** your email, display name, account type, and organization name if you post for an organization.
- **Posts:** the text, place, times and up to 4 food photos you publish.
- **Reports:** the post you reported and your reason.

## 2. How we use it
We use this information only to run the App: to show food posts, send the alerts you asked for, let posters manage their posts, and keep the App safe.

## 3. AI processing
Food photos you choose to analyze are sent to Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic to draft a post. Photos carry no location or camera details, and requests carry no name or account.

## 4. Where it is stored and for how long
Data is stored with Google Firebase. Posts and photos are deleted about 24 hours after a post ends. Account data is kept until you delete your account in Settings.

## 5. Your choices
You can turn alerts and location off at any time, change what you are alerted about, and delete your account in Settings > Account.
