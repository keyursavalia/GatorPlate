## Key points 
- This is a **demo prototype**, not an official San Francisco State University app or service.
- Food is shared by students and student organizations. **Eat at your own risk** and confirm ingredients and allergens with the person hosting the food.
- Food details (diet type, allergens, calories) can be **generated by AI and may be wrong**.
- Only post food that has been kept safe. Stay with it, and mark it gone when it runs out.
- Don't photograph people. Don't post anything that isn't edible food.

not verify your identity or your enrollment. Do not rely on the App for anything important.

## 3. Who can use it
You must be a current member of the SFSU community (student, student organization member, faculty, or
staff) and at least 18 years old, or otherwise permitted to use campus services by SFSU. You are
responsible for keeping your login details private and for activity under your account.

## 4. Posting food (for posters and student organizations)
By publishing a post, you confirm that:
1. The food is real, edible, and offered for free.
2. It has been handled and stored safely: hot food kept hot, cold food kept cold, and not left out for more than 2 hours.
3. You will stay with the food or make sure someone does, and you will mark the post as gone when the food runs out or the time ends.
4. You will not post alcohol, expired or visibly spoiled food, or anything unsafe.
5. The location you enter is accurate and on the SFSU campus.
6. You have reviewed all post details, including any AI-suggested text, and corrected anything wrong.
7. If you post for a student organization, you are authorized to do so for that organization.
Follow all SFSU rules and applicable food-safety guidance for events. Posts are time-limited (default 30
minutes, maximum 60 minutes) and are removed from view automatically when time ends.

## 5. Taking food (for everyone else)
- Taking food is **voluntary and at your own risk**. The App does not prepare, inspect, test, or guarantee any food.
- **Ask the host about ingredients and allergens** before eating. If you have allergies or dietary restrictions, do not rely on the App.
- Use your judgment: if food looks or smells wrong, do not eat it.
- Be considerate: take a fair share and thank the host.

## 6. AI-generated information
The App uses artificial intelligence (Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic) to look at a photo
you take and suggest a title, description, food items, diet type, possible allergens, calorie ranges, and
safety notes.
- AI output is **an estimate and can be incorrect or incomplete**. It may misidentify food, miss allergens, or mislabel a dish as vegan or vegetarian.
- Allergen information is shown as "may contain" and is **never a guarantee** that food is free of any ingredient.
- Calorie figures are rough ranges and are not nutrition or medical advice. You can hide them in Settings.
- Posters must review and are responsible for the final post content. AI does not publish anything on its own.
- What is sent to the AI: the photo you chose to analyze (with location metadata removed) and our instructions. We do not intentionally send your name, email, or location to the AI.

## 7. Photos and content
- Photograph **food only**. Do not photograph people, faces, IDs, or private information. If you accidentally do, retake the photo.
- You keep ownership of the content you post. You give the App a limited, non-exclusive permission to store and display it to other users for the purpose of operating the demo.
- Photos and posts are meant to be short-lived and are deleted after the post ends plus a short retention period (about 24 hours), though demo systems can fail to delete on schedule.

## 8. Location
The App can use your device's location while it is open to show where you are on the campus map and to
estimate walking directions. Your location stays on your device for this purpose and is not stored in
the App's database or shown to other users. You can turn off location in iOS Settings; the map will still
work without it. Directions may be provided through Apple Maps services subject to Apple's own terms.

## 9. Notifications

Settings or iOS Settings. Alerts may be delayed or may not arrive.
