## 4. Posting food
By publishing a post, you confirm that:
1. The food is real, edible, and offered for free, with nothing asked in return.
2. It has been handled and stored safely: hot food kept hot, cold food kept cold, and not left out for more than 2 hours.
3. You will stay with the food or make sure someone does, and you will mark the post as gone when the food runs out or the time ends.
4. You will not post alcohol, drugs, expired or visibly spoiled food, or anything unsafe.
5. The location you enter is accurate and on the SF State campus.
6. You have reviewed all post details, including any AI-suggested text, and corrected anything wrong.
7. If you post on behalf of a student organization, you are allowed to do so.

- Be considerate: take a fair share and thank the host.

## 6. AI-generated information and safety checks
The App uses artificial intelligence (Google's Gemini models) in two ways:
- **Drafting posts.** When you analyze food photos, they are sent through Firebase AI Logic so Gemini can suggest a title, description, food items, diet type, possible allergens, calorie ranges, and safety notes.
- **Safety checks.** Photos of published posts, and the reason written in an "Unsafe to eat" report together with the reported post, may be checked by Gemini through Google Cloud (Agent Platform) to find content that breaks these Terms.
What you should know:
- AI output is **an estimate and can be incorrect or incomplete**. It may misidentify food, miss allergens, or mislabel a dish as vegan or vegetarian.
- Allergen information is shown as "may contain" and is **never a guarantee** that food is free of any ingredient.
- Calorie figures are rough ranges and are not nutrition or medical advice. You can hide them in Settings.
- Posters must review and are responsible for the final post. AI never publishes anything on its own.
- We send photos with location data removed, the post's text where needed, and our instructions. We never send your name, email address, or account ID to the AI.

## 12. Reporting, blocking and moderation
- **Report** any post from its More menu as Unsafe to eat, Offensive or inappropriate, Spam or misleading, or Selling or trading. Reporting needs an account with a verified email. Reports are private: the poster is never told who reported them.
- **How removal works today:** a post is removed after 3 reports from different people, or at once when our AI check finds an "Unsafe to eat" report credible. We may adjust these limits.
- **Offensive reports** are reviewed by a person within 24 hours.
- **Automatic checks:** words that aren't allowed are blocked when you write a post or a name, and photos of published posts are checked automatically. A post that fails a check is removed.
- **Posting pauses:** repeated removals pause posting. Today, 4 removals within 30 days pause posting for 7 days. You can still browse, get alerts, report, and block.
- **Appeals:** you can appeal a pause in the App. A person reviews every appeal, and the App shows the result.
- **Block:** anyone, including guests, can block a poster from a post's More menu. You will no longer see their posts or get alerts for them. They are not told. We are notified when someone is blocked so we can review the poster. You can unblock people in Settings > Safety > Blocked people.
