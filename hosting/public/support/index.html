GatorPlate Support

GatorPlate is a free iOS app where people on the San Francisco State campus post leftover food from events, and everyone else gets notified and finds it on a campus map. It is an independent student project, not an official SF State app.

Contact

Email contact@keyursavalia.com. We read every message. Reports of offensive posts are reviewed by a person within 24 hours.

Report a post or block a poster

  • Open the post on the map, tap the More (⋯) button, then Report and pick a reason: Unsafe to eat, Offensive or inappropriate, Spam or misleading, or Selling or trading. Reporting needs an account with a verified email.
  • In the same menu, Block hides that poster's posts and alerts for you. They aren't told. Unblock people in Settings > Safety > Blocked people.

Delete your account

In the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes your account, your posts and photos, the reports you filed, your blocks, your appeal and your moderation records.
