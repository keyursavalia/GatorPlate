GatorPlate Terms of Use

Version: 2026-11-1 Last updated: October 20, 2026

Key points

  • GatorPlate is an independent app, not an official San Francisco State University app or service.
  • Food is shared by other people. Eat at your own risk and confirm ingredients and allergens with the person hosting the food.
  • Food details (diet type, allergens, calories) can be generated by AI and may be wrong.
  • Only post free food that has been kept safe. Stay with it, and mark it gone when it runs out.
  • Zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Report posts and block posters from a post's More menu.
  • Don't photograph people. Don't post anything that isn't edible food.

1. About this app

GatorPlate ("the App") is an independent service built by KEYUR DIPAKBHAI SAVALIA for the SF State community. It lets people on the SF State campus post and find leftover food. The App is not operated, endorsed, or sponsored by San Francisco State University. For the official program, visit basicneeds.sfsu.edu/gator-grub-alert.

2. The service

The App shows free food that people post on campus, sends alerts if you turn them on, and helps you find the food with Apple Maps.

  • Anyone can browse as a guest. Posting needs an account with a verified email address.
  • We check that you control the email address you sign up with. We do not verify your identity, your enrollment, or any connection to SF State.
  • We do not inspect, test, or vouch for any food, poster, or post.
  • Features may change, and the App may be unavailable at times.

3. Who can use it

You must be at least 17 years old. Anyone can create an account with an email address they can verify. You are responsible for keeping your login details private and for activity under your account.

4. Posting food

By publishing a post, you confirm that:

  1. The food is real, edible, and offered for free, with nothing asked in return.
  2. It has been handled and stored safely: hot food kept hot, cold food kept cold, and not left out for more than 2 hours.
  3. You will stay with the food or make sure someone does, and you will mark the post as gone when the food runs out or the time ends.
  4. You will not post alcohol, drugs, expired or visibly spoiled food, or anything unsafe.
  5. The location you enter is accurate and on the SF State campus.
  6. You have reviewed all post details, including any AI-suggested text, and corrected anything wrong.
  7. If you post on behalf of a student organization, you are allowed to do so.

Follow SF State rules and food-safety guidance for events. Posts are time-limited (default 30 minutes, maximum 60 minutes) and disappear automatically when time ends.

5. Taking food

  • Taking food is voluntary and at your own risk. The App does not prepare, inspect, test, or guarantee any food.
  • Ask the host about ingredients and allergens before eating. If you have allergies or dietary restrictions, do not rely on the App.
  • Use your judgment: if food looks or smells wrong, do not eat it.
  • Be considerate: take a fair share and thank the host.

6. AI-generated information and safety checks

The App uses artificial intelligence (Google's Gemini models) in two ways:

  • Drafting posts. When you analyze food photos, they are sent through Firebase AI Logic so Gemini can suggest a title, description, food items, diet type, possible allergens, calorie ranges, and safety notes.
  • Safety checks. Photos of published posts, and the reason written in an "Unsafe to eat" report together with the reported post, may be checked by Gemini through Google Cloud (Agent Platform) to find content that breaks these Terms.

What you should know:

  • AI output is an estimate and can be incorrect or incomplete. It may misidentify food, miss allergens, or mislabel a dish as vegan or vegetarian.
  • Allergen information is shown as "may contain" and is never a guarantee that food is free of any ingredient.
  • Calorie figures are rough ranges and are not nutrition or medical advice. You can hide them in Settings.
  • Posters must review and are responsible for the final post. AI never publishes anything on its own.
  • We send photos with location data removed, the post's text where needed, and our instructions. We never send your name, email address, or account ID to the AI.

7. Photos and content

  • Photograph food only. Do not photograph people, faces, IDs, or private information. If you accidentally do, retake the photo.
  • You keep ownership of the content you post. You give us a limited, non-exclusive permission to store it, check it, and show it to other users to operate the App.
  • Posts and photos are short-lived and are deleted about 24 hours after a post ends.

8. Location

The App can use your device's location while it is open to show where you are on the campus map and how far food is. Your location stays on your device: it is not stored in our database or shown to anyone. You can turn location off in iOS Settings; the map still works without it. Directions open in Apple Maps, which follows Apple's own terms.

9. Notifications

If you turn them on, the App alerts you about new food posts. We may also notify you about your own posts, for example when one is removed, or about an appeal. You can turn alerts off at any time in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings. Alerts may be delayed or may not arrive.

10. Privacy summary

We collect only what the App needs to work: an anonymous ID for guests; your email address and display name if you create an account; the posts, reports, blocks, and appeals you make; and your alert settings. We do not track who picks up food or build a history of what anyone takes, we show no ads, and we never sell data. Data is stored with Google Firebase. Details and retention periods are in the Privacy Policy.

11. Community guidelines and zero tolerance

GatorPlate has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not post, write, or upload:

  • harassment, hate, or discrimination against anyone;
  • sexual content;
  • photos of people;
  • threats or violence;
  • illegal items, alcohol, or drugs;
  • offers to sell or trade (all food must be free);
  • spam, or false or misleading posts.

Also do not collect data about other users, or try to break, overload, or get around the App's security. Breaking these rules gets content removed and can get your account suspended or removed.

12. Reporting, blocking and moderation

  • Report any post from its More menu as Unsafe to eat, Offensive or inappropriate, Spam or misleading, or Selling or trading. Reporting needs an account with a verified email. Reports are private: the poster is never told who reported them.
  • How removal works today: a post is removed after 3 reports from different people, or at once when our AI check finds an "Unsafe to eat" report credible. We may adjust these limits.
  • Offensive reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours.
  • Automatic checks: words that aren't allowed are blocked when you write a post or a name, and photos of published posts are checked automatically. A post that fails a check is removed.
  • Posting pauses: repeated removals pause posting. Today, 4 removals within 30 days pause posting for 7 days. You can still browse, get alerts, report, and block.
  • Appeals: you can appeal a pause in the App. A person reviews every appeal, and the App shows the result.
  • Block: anyone, including guests, can block a poster from a post's More menu. You will no longer see their posts or get alerts for them. They are not told. We are notified when someone is blocked so we can review the poster. You can unblock people in Settings > Safety > Blocked people.
