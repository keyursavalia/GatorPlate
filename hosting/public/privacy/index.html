GatorPlate Privacy Policy

Version: 2026-11-1 Last updated: October 20, 2026

Key points

  • You can browse GatorPlate and get alerts without an account. Guests get an anonymous ID, with no name or email.
  • An account (email and display name) is needed only to post food or report posts.
  • Your location stays on your phone. It is never stored or shared.
  • Photos are checked by Google's Gemini AI to draft posts and keep the App safe. We never send your name, email, or account ID to the AI.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

  • Guests: an anonymous ID created by Firebase, the Terms version you accepted, and your alert settings if you turn alerts on.
  • Accounts: your email address and display name, plus the organization name on older organization accounts.
  • Posts: the text, place, times, and up to 4 food photos you publish.
  • Reports you file: the post, the category, your reason if you wrote one, and a snapshot of the reported post (title, description, place, and the poster's name and account ID; no photos) so a person can review it after the post ends.
  • Blocks: who you blocked and the display name they had at the time.
  • Appeals: the message you write when you appeal a posting pause, and the result.
  • Moderation records: removed posts that count toward a posting pause (post, reason, and time), pause end dates, and how many people have blocked you.
  • Alert settings: whether alerts are on, quiet hours, and your custom notification choices (for example diet or allergens to avoid). Only you and our systems can read them.
  • Device data for notifications: a Firebase installation ID for your device, so alerts reach you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history, and we do not track which food you pick up. Map and Feed filters, and the posts you reported, are remembered on your phone only.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to run the App: to show food posts, send the alerts you asked for, let posters manage their posts, review reports and appeals, and keep the App safe. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we never use your alert settings to target anyone.

3. AI processing

The App sends some data to Google's Gemini AI models. Google processes it as our service provider:

  • When you analyze food photos while posting, the photos (with location and camera details removed) and our instructions are sent through Firebase AI Logic to draft your post.
  • When a post is published, its photos are checked through Google Cloud (Agent Platform) for content that isn't allowed, such as people, sexual content, violence, or things that aren't food. Post text is checked against a word list on our own servers, not by AI.
  • When someone reports a post as Unsafe to eat, their written reason is sent together with the post's cover photo and text (title, description, food items, listed allergens and notes) to check whether the report is credible.

We never send names, email addresses, or account IDs to the AI.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud store the data and run the App's servers, sign-in, and AI requests. Firebase Remote Config fetches app settings, Firebase App Check verifies that requests come from the genuine App, and Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications.
  • Apple delivers notifications to your device, and Apple Maps opens directions when you ask for them.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we never sell or share data with data brokers.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account data and alert settings: until you delete your account.
  • Posts and photos: deleted about 24 hours after a post ends.
  • Reports: kept 90 days for review, then deleted automatically. Reports you filed are deleted at once if you delete your account.
  • Blocks: until you unblock the person, or either account is deleted.
  • Appeals: until the posting pause ends, then deleted automatically; deleted at once if you delete your account.
  • Moderation records: removed posts count for 30 days; older entries are dropped the next time the record changes. Records are deleted with your account.
  • Device installation ID: until you turn alerts off, sign out, or delete your account.
  • Server logs: they record counts and categories (never post text, names, or emails) and are kept up to 30 days.

Automatic deletions can take up to a day longer to run.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Settings > Account > Delete account. This deletes your account, your posts and photos, the reports you filed, your blocks (and blocks other people made of you), your appeal, and your moderation records. Reports other people filed about your posts are kept until they expire after 90 days.

7. Your choices

You can turn alerts and location off at any time, change what you are alerted about, block or unblock posters, and delete your account in Settings.

8. Age

GatorPlate is not meant for anyone under 17, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the version above, and the App asks you to accept the new Terms and Privacy Policy.
